# **Privacy Policy for Einstein’s Spacetime**

*Effective Date: November 7, 2024*

Thank you for using Einstein’s Spacetime, an educational VR game designed to bring immersive learning experiences without compromising your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data practices and your rights regarding any data collected by the app.

### 1. Information We Collect
Einstein’s Spacetime does not collect, store, or process any personal or usage data about its users. We designed our app to operate fully without needing access to any personal information or device data.

### 2. How We Use User Data
Since we do not collect or store any user data, there is no personal information or user activity data being used or processed by Einstein’s Spacetime.

### 3. Data Deletion and User Requests
Because Einstein’s Spacetime does not collect or store any data, there is no user data to delete. If you have any questions about this policy or your data privacy, please feel free to reach out to us at phys-pointvr@illinois.edu.

### 4. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or industry standards. If we make any updates, the revised policy will be posted here with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If you have further questions, please contact us at phys-pointvr@illinois.edu.
